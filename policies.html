Journal policies

How we handle ownership, ethics, AI use, and disputes.

Authors own their work

All research published here is written by its credited authors. Copying, republishing, or claiming it as your own is not permitted.

Copyright & Ownership

Every paper in this journal is the original work of its author or authors, who retain full copyright. STEMinate India does not own the papers and publishes them with the authors’ permission.

  • No paper may be copied, reproduced, redistributed, or republished, in whole or in part, without the author’s written permission.
  • You may share a link to a paper on this site. Do not re-upload it elsewhere or present it as your own work.
  • If you quote or build on a paper, cite the author and this journal.
Authorship & Submissions

This journal does not accept public submissions. It publishes work by members of the STEMinate India Research Team.

  • Only people who made a real contribution to the research and writing are credited as authors.
  • Authors are responsible for the accuracy of their data, claims, and references.
Publication Ethics

Authors must report their research honestly.

  • Do not fabricate, falsify, or selectively report data.
  • Cite every source, dataset, and piece of prior work you use.
  • Get informed consent from any human participants, and never publish their personal details.
  • Do not publish the same work in more than one place without disclosing it.
Plagiarism Policy

Copying text, ideas, figures, or data without credit is not accepted. This includes close paraphrasing and reusing your own earlier work without disclosure.

Papers are checked for originality before publication. If plagiarism is found after publication, the paper is corrected or retracted.

AI Policy

AI tools may support tasks such as grammar checking or brainstorming. They cannot replace the author’s own research and writing.

  • AI cannot be credited as an author.
  • Authors are fully responsible for everything in their paper, including content an AI tool helped produce.
  • Do not present AI-generated text, data, images, or citations as original work. Check that every citation points to a real source.
  • Tell the editors about any meaningful use of AI.
Retraction & Corrections

Honest mistakes that do not change a paper’s main conclusions are fixed with a correction note on the paper.

Serious problems, such as plagiarism or fabricated data, lead to a retraction. A retracted paper stays visible and is clearly marked. Authors can respond before a decision is made.

Conflict of Interest

Authors and reviewers must disclose any personal, academic, or financial relationship that could influence the research or its review. Reviewers do not review their own work or the work of close friends or family.

Privacy

This website does not collect personal data from visitors. Author names and paper details are published only with the author’s consent. To have your information changed or removed, contact us.

Reporting a Concern

If you believe a paper copies your work, breaks these policies, or contains an error, email us with the paper’s title and a description of your concern. We will review it and reply.
